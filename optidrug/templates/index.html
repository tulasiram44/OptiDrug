Molecular Descriptors Utilized

• Molecular Weight (MW): Lipinski cutoff $\le 500\text{ Da}$, controls steric accessibility to kinase cleft.
• $\text{LogP}$ (Lipophilicity): Octanol-water partition coefficient, dictates membrane permeability and hydrophobic binding.
• $\text{HBD}$ & $\text{HBA}$ (Hydrogen Bond Donors/Acceptors): Dictates electrostatic anchoring with hinge residues (e.g. Met793 in EGFR).
• $\text{TPSA}$ (Polar Surface Area): Veber limit $\le 140\text{ \AA}^2$, predictive of oral bioavailability and cellular influx.
• Rotatable Bonds: Veber limit $\le 10$, governs entropic loss upon receptor-ligand complexation.
